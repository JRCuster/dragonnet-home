DragonNet Home Assistant

DragonNet Home Assistant is a private link between its owner's own Home Assistant, the system that runs his home automation, and his own Google account. It is not a public service.

What it does

  • Shows the owner's own Google Tasks lists inside his own Home Assistant.
  • Lets the owner add, change and complete his own tasks from there.

Who can use it

Only its owner. There are no accounts to sign up for, no downloads and no other users. The link is built on the open-source Home Assistant program and runs only on the owner's own home server.

Privacy

The app collects, shares and stores no data. Read the privacy policy.
