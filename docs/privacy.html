DragonNet Home Assistant

Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026

DragonNet Home Assistant collects no data, shares no data and stores no data. It only accesses its owner's own Google Tasks.

What the app is

DragonNet Home Assistant is a private link between its owner's own Home Assistant, the system that runs his home automation, and his own Google account. It is not a public service and has no other users.

Google user data the app accesses

The app signs in only to its owner's own Google account and uses the Google Tasks API to:

  • read the owner's own task lists and tasks, to show them in his own Home Assistant;
  • add, change and complete tasks when the owner does so there.

It does not access any other Google data, such as Gmail, Contacts, Drive or Photos.

Collection, sharing and storage

  • No collection. The app has no server of its own, no analytics, no tracking and no telemetry. Nothing is sent to the app's maintainer or to anyone else.
  • No sharing. No data is shared with, sold to or transferred to any third party.
  • No storage by the app. Tasks stay in the owner's own Google account. The Google sign-in token stays on the owner's own home server and is used only to reach his Google Tasks.

Google API Services User Data Policy

DragonNet Home Assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from his Google Account, under Security → Third-party apps & services.

Changes and contact

Any change to this policy will be published on this page. Questions can be raised through @JRCuster on GitHub.
